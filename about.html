The Visionary Leader

Message from The Proprietress

"When we founded Wisdom and Knowledge School in Adebayo over two decades ago, our goal was simple: to create a citadel of learning where academic excellence and character moulding meet."

Every child has greatness embedded within them. As educators and parents, our sacred obligation is to provide the fertile soil, the intellectual stimulus, and the moral compass to guide them towards leadership.

Today, our campus on Off Housing Road, Adebayo Street boasts smart classrooms, hygienic cafeteria services, expansive sports grounds, and science laboratories that rank among the finest in the region. We welcome you to experience this rich learning journey with us.

Dr. (Mrs.) Folashade Adebayo, F.IoD
